SnackScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

SnackScout is a scouting app made by and for FIRST Tech Challenge Team 34672, Snack Time. It helps our team members record and share observations about robots at competitions. This page explains what information SnackScout uses and how it is handled.

Who can use SnackScout

SnackScout is only for members of our team. Creating an account requires a team code from the team.

Information we collect

  • Account details you enter: a display name, a username, and a password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form).
  • If you choose "Sign in with Google": your Google account's name, email address, and Google account ID. SnackScout asks Google only for basic profile information (the openid, email and profile scopes). It never sees your Google password and has no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • Scouting you record: notes and counts about robots and matches, with your display name attached so teammates know who scouted it.
  • Display preferences: such as your chosen color theme.
  • A login cookie that keeps you signed in on your device. SnackScout uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • Information from Google is used only to sign you in and to show which teammate made which scouting entry.
  • Scouting data is used only by our team to plan for competitions.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside the team, and we do not use it for advertising.

SnackScout's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on the team's own computer running SnackScout and, if the team turns on its "team cloud", in the team's private database hosted by Supabase. Only the team's SnackScout software can read that database. Public robotics data (scores, rankings and match videos) comes from FTCScout, the FIRST Tech Challenge Events API and YouTube.

Your choices

  • You can unlink Google at any time in SnackScout under Settings → Account.
  • You can also remove SnackScout's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your account or information deleted, ask a SnackScout admin on Team Snack Time. Admins can remove accounts in the app.

Children's privacy

Team members may be students. SnackScout collects only what is listed above and nothing else, and it is used only within the team.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
